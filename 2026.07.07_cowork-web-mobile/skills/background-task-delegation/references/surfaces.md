# Where Cowork runs, and what each surface is for

Cowork sessions and files go where you go, on any device. The surfaces are not interchangeable
though — each one is good at a different phase of the work.

| Surface | Role | How to get in |
|---|---|---|
| **Desktop** | The place for deep work. The full Cowork experience, where Claude can also use your **local files and browser**. | Download the Claude desktop app. |
| **Web** | Start and check on sessions without installing anything. | Start a Cowork session from the home screen at claude.ai. |
| **Mobile** | Check on running work and answer the questions that reach you. | Open Cowork from the sidebar of the Claude app for iOS and Android. |

For what is available on each surface, see the Help Center.

## What the expansion changed

Until this release, Cowork lived on your laptop, so the work stopped when you stepped away. Three
things changed:

### Your work follows you

Start a task at your desk, check on it from your phone, and pick up the finished output anywhere.

### Work continues in the background

Close the laptop and head to your meeting; Claude keeps going. **Scheduled tasks now run with no
device online** — nothing has to stay awake for the work to happen.

### The decisions still come to you

When Claude reaches a call only you can make, it asks, and the question reaches your phone. You can
redirect a draft mid-meeting and Claude keeps going, on the right path. **Nothing ships until you
have reviewed and approved it.**

## Two more changes

- On web and desktop, **chat and Cowork now share one home**, and your projects and artifacts live
  together across both.
- **Delegating is now as easy as asking** — the handoff is a normal conversation rather than a
  separate mode you have to set up.

Users who could not install a desktop app can now use Cowork too.

## Practical consequence for how you write a brief

Because questions will reach you on a phone, mid-meeting, write briefs that are steerable in one
short reply. State the outcome and the constraints you are sure about; let the genuinely open calls
arrive as questions rather than trying to pre-answer every branch in the brief.

## Availability

Beta access rolled out over the several weeks following the announcement, starting with Max users,
with more plans to follow. Doubled Cowork usage limits were extended through August 5, 2026 to mark
the launch.
