## What is this post?
Claude Cowork is rolling out to mobile and web, so sessions and files go where you go on any device. The post opens with the usage data behind the decision: more than 90% of Cowork usage was not software development. Most of it was everyday knowledge work, with business operations and content creation together making up roughly half — reconciling the quarter's spend and drafting the variance memo, turning a folder of contracts into a renewals tracker with the risks flagged, building tomorrow's client deck from call transcripts and pipeline data. The post calls this "the work around the work": rarely in anyone's job description, but a large share of everyone's week.

The argument for the expansion follows from the shape of that work. Asking AI for an answer and handing it the work are different things, and handed-over work doesn't fit in one sitting — it accumulates overnight, between meetings, on the train. Until this release Cowork lived on your laptop, so the work stopped when you stepped away. Three things change: your work follows you across devices, work continues in the background with scheduled tasks running with no device online, and the decisions still come to you — questions reach your phone, and nothing ships until you've reviewed and approved it.

## When is it useful?
- When a task accumulates over days rather than finishing in one sitting, and you keep losing it when you step away from the laptop.
- When you want work waiting for you at the start of the day rather than starting it then.
- When you'll be moving between desk, phone, and web while a task is running.
- When output must not leave the building without your review, and you need the approval line stated explicitly.
- When someone can't install a desktop app and you need to know what they still get.
- When writing a delegation brief and it keeps coming out as a list of steps instead of a description of done.

## Key points
- **Over 90% of Cowork usage was not software development.** Business operations and content creation are the largest categories, together roughly half of all usage.
- **The three named examples** are the reconciliation-plus-variance-memo, the contracts folder turned renewals tracker with risks flagged, and the client deck built from transcripts and pipeline data.
- **Your work follows you.** Start at your desk, check from your phone, pick up the finished output anywhere.
- **Work continues in the background.** Close the laptop and Claude keeps going. Scheduled tasks now run with **no device online** — the 6 am Monday client prep builds the briefing doc and leaves the follow-up email drafted but unsent.
- **The decisions still come to you.** When Claude reaches a call only you can make, it asks, and the question reaches your phone. You can redirect a draft mid-meeting and Claude keeps going on the right path. Nothing ships until you've reviewed and approved it.
- **Desktop is still the place for deep work** — the full experience, where Claude can also use your local files and browser. Web and mobile are where you start, check in, and decide.
- **Chat and Cowork now share one home** on web and desktop, with projects and artifacts living together across both. Delegating is now as easy as asking.
- **The starting instruction is a two-part brief:** point Claude at the folder, the thread, or the half-finished deck, and describe what done looks like. The second half is what turns supervision into delegation.
- **Users who couldn't install a desktop app can now use Cowork too.** Beta rolled out over the following weeks starting with Max users; doubled usage limits ran through August 5, 2026.
