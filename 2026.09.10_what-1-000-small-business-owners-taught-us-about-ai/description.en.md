## What is this post?
A recap (September 10, 2026) by Lina Ochman, Head of U.S. SMB at Anthropic, of six weeks of free half-day
workshops in ten U.S. cities, run with partner Tenex after the May launch of Claude for Small Business (a
Claude Cowork plugin built with QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot, Canva, DocuSign, and others) and the AI Fluency
for Small Businesses course made with PayPal. More than 1,000 owners and operators attended.

## When is it useful?
- When a small business owner (often non-technical) wants to get a first real workflow running in Claude.
- When deciding how to check AI output and handle data and governance in a small company.
- When a chamber of commerce, consultant, or trainer plans hands-on AI workshops for local businesses.

## Key points
- AI levels the field: owners of 5-50 person firms in construction, manufacturing, logistics, trades, and
  professional services built their own tools, such as bid prep from blueprints and shop-floor part identification.
- Requests were about running the business (about two-thirds) and growing it (about a third); reporting was the
  top use case. Treat Claude like a new employee and check its work, e.g. ask it to show its reasoning.
- In a survey of 503 decision-makers, data security was the most-cited barrier. Owners kept a human in the loop
  for review and sending; some added PII redaction and workflow registries. Team and Enterprise plans don't
  train on data by default.
- Agents feel intimidating until the first workflow runs: many had used chat but not Cowork, skills, or
  connectors. The guided build session was the most valued part; nearly two-thirds asked for more hands-on help.
- Owners learn best from peers: local partners hosted, and attendees wanted a shared repository of peer use cases
  more than any feature. Next: the Claude SMB Trainer Program (starting in San Francisco and New York City) and a
  second tour from Boston on September 16.
