# Review checklist: treat Claude like a new employee

Based on the workshop lessons in the source post.

- [ ] Did Claude show its reasoning for numbers, totals, and conclusions?
- [ ] Spot-check at least one figure against the original data.
- [ ] Are the assumptions stated and correct for this business?
- [ ] Is a person reviewing before anything is sent to a customer, supplier, or employee?
- [ ] Does the output include data it should not (PII that could be redacted)?
- [ ] Is the workflow recorded in the registry with a named reviewer?
