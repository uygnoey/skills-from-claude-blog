# Template — weekly marketing metrics review

The initial report contains the metrics tables and the suggested headlines. Narrative
expansion happens only after a human confirms which headline is the story.

```markdown
# Weekly marketing review — week of <date>

## Suggested areas of focus
<!-- Three to five candidates, one line each, with the reason it stood out.
     Do not expand any of these until one is confirmed. -->
1. <headline> — <why it stood out this week>
2. <headline> — <why it stood out this week>
3. <headline> — <why it stood out this week>

## Metrics
| Metric | This week | Last week | Δ | Source | Verified |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <name> | <value> | <value> | <±%> | <dashboard / warehouse / Slack thread / transcript> | <yes / no> |

## Mismatches requiring a decision
<!-- Two figures for the same thing, from different sources. State both, state the
     grouping or definition that differs, and ask. Do not reconcile. -->
- <metric>: <figure A> (<source A>) vs <figure B> (<source B>).
  Differing definition: <what>. **Question:** <which should the report use?>

---

## Narrative
<!-- Written after the human confirms the focus. Only the confirmed headline is
     expanded; the others stay as one-liners above. -->

### <confirmed headline>
<what changed, with the supporting detail and examples>

## Leadership slide
<!-- Generated from the same data and narrative above. -->
- **What changed:** <one line>
- **Why:** <one line>
- **What the teams are doing about it:** <one line>

## Follow-ups
<!-- Each becomes a task in the tracker, with enough context to act on alone. -->
- [ ] <action> — <owner> — <context>

## For the skills
<!-- End-of-session capture: what should go back into the skill files. -->
- <structural change, correction, or framing preference to encode>
```

## Quarter-turn variant

At the quarter turn, the quarterly review doc is an input and the report leads with
quarterly plans. Move the `## Narrative` section above `## Metrics`, open it with the
quarterly plan, and treat weekly movement as supporting material.
