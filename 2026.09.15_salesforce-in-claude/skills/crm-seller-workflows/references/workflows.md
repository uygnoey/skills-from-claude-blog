# Seller workflows (as described in the source)

| Workflow | Inputs | Outputs | Writes (after approval) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning brief (scheduled, background, available in the Claude app) | Calendar meetings, Salesforce pipeline, unread threads | Meetings, deals closing soon, at-risk opportunities, threads needing replies; Friday week summary + manager update draft | Close date, stage, follow-up tasks |
| Call prep | Salesforce, Slack, email | Open opportunities, recent account-team discussion, unanswered threads, open questions from prior calls | New contacts for stakeholders missing from Salesforce |
| Deal review + close plan | Opportunity, team methodology | Score, qualification gaps, unmet stakeholders, close-date risks; business case; dated mutual close plan | Qualification fields, contact roles, plan saved to opportunity |
| Post-meeting | Transcript or notes | Follow-up email, Slack deal-channel summary | Next steps, stage, close date |
| Pipeline + forecast | Pipeline across accounts | Interactive, shareable dashboard (coverage by stage, slip risk and why, account drill-downs); forecast narrative in leadership's format | Close dates, stages from the dashboard |

## Checklists

### Call prep
- [ ] Open opportunities for the account
- [ ] Recent account-team discussion in Slack
- [ ] Unanswered email threads
- [ ] Open questions from prior calls
- [ ] Stakeholders in threads but not in Salesforce -> propose as contacts

### Deal review
- [ ] Score against the team's methodology
- [ ] Qualification gaps
- [ ] Stakeholders not yet met
- [ ] Close-date risks
- [ ] Business case draft
- [ ] Dated mutual close plan
- [ ] Missing qualification fields and contact roles
