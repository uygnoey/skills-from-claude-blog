# Intent: <feature name>

Author: <name> (<team>). Status: draft.

## Problem

What can customers or colleagues not do today? Describe it in your own words — no formal
language required. Include the cost of the problem if you know it.

## Proposed outcome

What should change. Written as the state of the world after the change, not as an
implementation.

## Affected users and systems

Who is affected and which systems or teams are in scope.

## Constraints

Limitations that any solution has to respect — data handling, authentication, existing
contracts, regulatory requirements, anything explicitly out of scope.

## Open questions

Everything still unresolved. These carry forward into `spec.md` and must be answered or
explicitly carried again.

---

**How to use this template**

1. Brainstorm with Claude until the idea is concrete. Claude asks the questions an analyst
   would ask: scope, users, constraints, and what success looks like.
2. Ask Claude to write the result into this structure.
3. Correct anything Claude misunderstood — you are the author of record.
4. Commit to the shared intent home. Author and timestamp join the record, and the product
   owner picks it up from there.

Encode this template as a skill so every contributor produces the same shape without being
told. A technical team member writes the skill; a lead signs it off.
