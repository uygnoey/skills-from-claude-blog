## Patterns to reuse
1. **Voice to-do triage** — talk through the list in the single thread; let Duet split it into tasks.
2. **End-of-day handoff** — give the list before leaving; ask for an evening report.
3. **Calendar watchdog** — monitoring catches conflicts (wrong day, call before a flight) without
   being asked.
4. **Multi-location back office** — accounting, support, marketing, and per-store invoices, with
   payments held for approval.
5. **Meeting prep** — a brief prepared ahead of a client call.
