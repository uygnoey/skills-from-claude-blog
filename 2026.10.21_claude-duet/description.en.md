## What is this post?
The research-preview launch of Claude Duet, a proactive agent app for professionals and small
business owners, built on Claude Opus 5.5. It sends a morning brief, monitors connected apps all day,
and takes on tasks in the background on its own virtual machine.

## When is it useful?
- When a US Pro or Max user wants to decide what to delegate to Duet.
- When explaining what Duet does on its own versus what waits for approval.
- When setting expectations about the preview's limits and usage cost.

## Key points
- Three modes: morning brief, monitoring (messages only when something needs attention), and tasks
  from scheduling to dashboards, forecasts, prototypes, and web apps.
- Memory, skills, and connected apps carry over from the Claude account; one ongoing thread by text
  or voice; checks accounts every few hours, with user-chosen topics and frequency.
- Reads, drafts, and prepares on its own; sending, paying, signing, or deleting waits for approval.
  Some actions (trading securities, moving money between accounts) are off-limits even with approval.
- Safety: trustworthy-agents framework, Managed Agents credential design (never sees passwords or
  tokens), auto mode classifier, prompt-injection defenses, red teaming, external pen test, bug
  bounty, and a sensitive-data classifier.
- 6,000+ Marketplace connectors; purchases via Link virtual cards or Shop Pay.
- Limits: may misjudge urgency, weaker at personal errands, cannot show its browser yet, separate
  app, background work counts toward usage limits.
- Research preview for US Pro and Max via waitlist; iOS, Android, and claude.ai.
