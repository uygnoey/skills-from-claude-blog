## What is this post?
An announcement (September 10, 2026) that T. Rowe Price, a global active investment manager, is expanding
Claude across its investment organization. Portfolio managers and analysts use Claude and Claude Cowork for
research; developers use Claude Code to build investment tools.

## When is it useful?
- When an asset manager or other research-driven firm plans to bring AI to front-office decision makers
  rather than starting in the back office.
- When deciding which research and process work to hand to Claude while keeping human judgment central.
- When designing ownership and governance for AI applications inside business units.

## Key points
- Three use areas: research synthesis in Cowork (freeing time for analysis and debate), knowledge-intensive
  multi-step processes in Cowork where consistency and oversight matter, and tool building with Claude Code.
- Investment decisions still rest on the firm's own fundamental research and judgment; Claude adds capacity.
- Governance follows the AI leadership model announced in August 2026: AI leaders sit inside Investments and
  Global Distribution, T. Rowe Price Labs evaluates and scales AI firm-wide, the business owns each
  application, and responsible-use standards govern it.
- Anthropic notes the deployment started with the people who pick securities, unlike most enterprise rollouts.
- Broader effort includes training, talent development, and responsible-use guidance.
