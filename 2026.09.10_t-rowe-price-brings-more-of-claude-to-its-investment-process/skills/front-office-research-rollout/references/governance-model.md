# Governance model (as described in the source)

T. Rowe Price runs its Claude work under the AI leadership model it announced in August 2026.

| Element | What the post says |
|---|---|
| Embedded AI leaders | AI leaders sit inside Investments and Global Distribution. |
| Central hub | T. Rowe Price Labs, the enterprise AI and data innovation hub, evaluates and scales AI across the firm. |
| Ownership | The business owns each application. |
| Standards | The firm's responsible-use standards govern each application. |
| Accountability | Human judgment and accountability remain central to client outcomes. |
| Enablement | Training, talent development, and responsible-use guidance. |

## Adapting it
- Map "embedded AI leaders" to the business lines that will use Claude first.
- Map the "central hub" to whichever team evaluates and scales AI firm-wide.
- Make ownership explicit per application in the use-case register.

Specific policies, approval steps, and tooling are not described in the post; see the original.
