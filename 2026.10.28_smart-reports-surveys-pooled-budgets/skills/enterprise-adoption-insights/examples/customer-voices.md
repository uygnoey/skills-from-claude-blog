# Customer voices (paraphrased)

The post quotes six early users of smart reports. Summaries below are paraphrased; see the original for
exact wording.

| Organization (logo in post) | Role | What smart reports gave them |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Head of Applied AI | A grounded company-wide view of Claude use across several initiatives, to prioritize next steps |
| Slalom | VP, Business Transformation | Visibility into real AI use, turned into action: high-value use cases, enablement strategy, focused learning |
| Wix | Software Engineer | Reports built on tens of thousands of sessions that surface top pain points and workflows worth making into skills |
| Deloitte | Managing Director, AI Transformation | Enterprise-wide visibility to inform deployment and governance; early insights on effective workflows and emerging skills |
| DoorDash | AI Enablement Lead, Engineering Enablement | Surfaced things even a close observer missed; a go-to tool for conversations with leaders about where teams thrive or need support |
| Docusign | (not named in post) | Visibility from coding its AI-native contracting platform to everyday work; showing leadership outcomes, fixing connector bottlenecks, scaling what works |

## Patterns worth copying
- Use reports to pick the next enablement priorities, not just to count usage.
- Mine reports for recurring workflows and turn them into skills.
- Use connector friction in reports as a fix list.
- Bring report findings to leadership conversations about outcomes.
