# Smart reports

Summary of what the source says about smart reports. Setup details: the Help Center article
"Get started with smart reports" (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16893491-get-started-with-smart-reports).

## What a report shows
- What a team gets done with Claude and what that work costs.
- Ambitious work the team is doing and how Claude made it possible.
- How to turn that work into repeatable workflows for the rest of the organization.

## Inputs
| Input | Notes |
|---|---|
| Team | The group to analyze |
| Date range | The window to sample |
| Products | Which Claude products to include |

Claude analyzes a **sample** of the team's transcripts and groups the work into **workstreams**.

## Per-workstream output
- Spend for each workstream.
- Deliverables produced.
- Most common friction points, such as a missing connector or a failed tool call.

## Recommendations
- One consolidated list per report.
- Includes recurring workflows to turn into skills and rules to add to the code repository.
- Any finding can be shared as an artifact with the people who can act on it.

## Custom questions and templates
- Reports answer custom questions about model usage and cost.
- Admins can save questions and custom insights as templates to reuse in future reports.

## Permissions
| Who | Can |
|---|---|
| Owners | Let team leads who aren't admins run reports for their own groups |
| Admins with billing permission | Raise a spend limit directly from the report |
| Delegated team leads | Run reports for their own groups |

## Intended use
- Understand adoption and inform investment planning.
- **Not** for evaluating individual performance or making employment decisions.
