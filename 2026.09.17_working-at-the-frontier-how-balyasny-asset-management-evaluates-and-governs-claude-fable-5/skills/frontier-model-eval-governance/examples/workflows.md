# Workflow examples from the BAM post

## Merger-arbitrage deal analysis
1. Deal is announced.
2. Agent estimates likelihood and timing of closing.
3. Extracts key economic and legal terms.
4. Identifies conditions and milestones.
5. Flags areas requiring investor judgment.
6. Human reviews before relying on the output.

Timing: previously three to five days; now under a day, with the agent running about 30 minutes.

## Tax-loss harvesting analysis (BAMAgent)
- Agent explored roughly 90,000 database tables, located the relevant mutual fund holdings data, and built
  its own weighting system.
- After team review, the result was more comprehensive than a traditional approach. No timing given.

## Recurring central-bank analysis (Chief Economist)
- Configured as an agent workflow.
- Roughly two days -> about 30 minutes; the economist keeps review and judgment.

## Scale
- BAMAgent (built over about six months) supports thousands of autonomous agents running around the clock.
- Some teams run more than 300 agents continuously analyzing new data.
- Fable is preferred for planning and analysis because errors there carry into every later deliverable.
