# Case studies (summarized from the source)

| Customer | What they build | Partner and product | Purchase via Claude Marketplace | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Power Digital** (marketing agency) | Nova platform; client data on Snowflake; Claude via Snowflake Cortex AI within existing governance | Snowflake | Expanded its Snowflake commitment (5-year Snowflake customer) | Spend from already-approved budget; teams scale Snowflake usage quickly; "contract was already being drawn up" by the time they asked. Claude use grew from ~12 engineers in January to 800+ people |
| **ThoughtSpot** (agentic analytics) | AI features running Claude against customer data inside Snowflake; Spotter is a connector for Claude | Snowflake inference API | Applied the purchase to its existing Anthropic commitment | Product team started building on budget already set aside; wants to sell its own agentic products through the marketplace |
| **CodeRabbit** (validates AI-generated code) | Agents write and test code in Vercel Sandbox (isolated Linux microVMs); Vercel Workflows coordinates long-running jobs that pause/resume | Vercel | Moved from pay-as-you-go to a committed plan, funded from a recently expanded Anthropic commitment | Deal closed within a week |

## Seller perspective
- **Snowflake**: Claude runs directly in Snowflake, so governance, context, and permissions are in place
  from day one; customers can direct part of their Anthropic commitment to Snowflake for AI workloads.
- **Vercel**: the marketplace lets customers like CodeRabbit quickly procure Vercel's agentic
  infrastructure.
- For sellers, the marketplace reaches customers already building on Claude and gives those with an
  Anthropic commitment a faster way to buy.
