## What is this post?
An announcement-style set of customer stories (September 23, 2026): CodeRabbit expanded its Vercel plan
through Claude Marketplace, and Power Digital and ThoughtSpot expanded their Snowflake usage, all by
applying part of their existing Anthropic commitment.

## When is it useful?
- When a team with an Anthropic commitment wants to expand a data or infrastructure vendor it already uses with Claude.
- When a new budget request would slow down a needed tool upgrade.
- When a vendor building on Claude is weighing selling through Claude Marketplace.

## Key points
- Claude Marketplace lets companies put part of an Anthropic commitment toward partner tools, so one
  investment covers more without a new budget request.
- Power Digital: runs client data on Snowflake (Claude via Snowflake Cortex AI); expanded its Snowflake
  commitment through the marketplace; Claude use grew from ~12 engineers to 800+ people.
- ThoughtSpot: runs Claude on customer data via Snowflake's inference API and applied that purchase to its
  Anthropic commitment; its AI analyst Spotter is a Claude connector.
- CodeRabbit: agents run code in Vercel Sandbox microVMs coordinated by Vercel Workflows; it moved from
  pay-as-you-go to a committed Vercel plan funded by its Anthropic commitment, closing within a week.
- Snowflake and Vercel see the marketplace as a faster way to reach customers already building on Claude.
- Using a commitment this way is in limited preview; talk to your account team or submit a request.
