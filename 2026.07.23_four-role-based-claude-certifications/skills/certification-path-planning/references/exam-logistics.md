# How the exams work

## Delivery

Anthropic works with **Pearson**, drawing on its global assessment and credentialing infrastructure,
to deliver the program.

- Every exam is **proctored** — taken under supervision.
- Exams are delivered through **Pearson Professional Assessments** and securely administered.
- Test takers must **validate their identity** before beginning an exam.
- Those who pass receive a **digital badge through Credly by Pearson**.

## Why proctoring and identity verification matter

These credentials are used to make decisions:

- Certified practitioner counts determine a firm's standing in the Claude Partner Network.
- The counts are relevant when companies vet who will work on their systems.

A credential can only support those decisions if the assessment is rigorous and validates a person's
applied skills. That is the reason the program uses supervised, identity-verified exams rather than
course-completion badges.

## Access and preparation

- Certification **preparation courses for each credential** are available in the Anthropic Partner
  Academy, Anthropic's free training platform for partners.
- **Exams are available to members of the Claude Partner Network.**
- Firms can join the Claude Partner Network and register practitioners at
  <http://claude.com/partners>. Membership is free.

## Scale so far

- Since launching in March 2026, more than **36,000 consultants** have received certification across
  more than **1,300 organizations**.
- More than **400,000 people** have completed Claude training this year through Anthropic Partner
  Academy.

## Tier review timing

Certifications determine how firms advance in the Claude Partner Network. The top tier, **Global
Premier**, requires:

- 1,000 certified practitioners
- 100 customers across three regions
- 15 public customer endorsements

The next tier review, where this criteria is evaluated across the network, is on **October 1**.
