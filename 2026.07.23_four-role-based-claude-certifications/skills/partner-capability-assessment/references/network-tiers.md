# Claude Partner Network standing and stated commitments

## Program context

- The Claude Partner Network launched in **March 2026**, alongside a **$100 million investment**.
- A **Services Track** followed in **June 2026**, to help enterprise buyers find the right firms to
  put Claude to work inside their business.
- Membership is **free**. Firms join and register practitioners at <http://claude.com/partners>.
- **Exams are available to members of the Claude Partner Network.**

## Tier standing

Certifications determine how firms advance in the network. The top tier, **Global Premier**,
requires:

- **1,000 certified practitioners**
- **100 customers across three regions**
- **15 public customer endorsements**

The next tier review, where this criteria is evaluated across the network, is on **October 1**.

## Program-wide delivered figures (as of July 2026)

- More than **36,000 consultants** certified, across more than **1,300 organizations**, since the
  March 2026 launch.
- More than **400,000 people** have completed Claude training this year through **Anthropic Partner
  Academy**, the free training platform for partners.
