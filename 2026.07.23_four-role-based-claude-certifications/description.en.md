## What is this post?
Anthropic expands the Claude Certification Program with three new role-based certifications, bringing the total to four. The credentials map to the four largest roles involved in putting Claude into production at a large enterprise: everyday practical use (Associate: Foundations), building applications with the Claude API, tool use, and agent development (Developer: Foundations), designing and building agent systems (Architect: Foundations), and integration architecture, governance, and evaluation at enterprise scale (Architect: Professional).

The argument running through the post is about what a credential has to be to be worth anything. Anthropic wants Claude certifications recognized as verified expertise rather than a badge, so every exam is proctored and identity-verified, delivered through Pearson Professional Assessments, and designed by subject matter experts — including Anthropic's own Applied AI team — to reflect real field work. That rigor exists because the credentials get used for decisions: certified practitioner counts set a firm's standing in the Claude Partner Network, and buyers use them to vet who will work on their systems.

## When is it useful?
- When deciding which certification a consultant, engineer, or architect on a Claude engagement should sit for.
- When a firm has committed to a certification number and needs that number turned into a mix of credentials.
- When selecting a delivery partner and reading their certification claims.
- When a firm cites a headline certification total and you need to know what it actually establishes.
- When planning a firm's advance through the Claude Partner Network tiers ahead of a review date.
- When preparing people for a proctored, identity-verified exam and its scheduling requirements.

## Key points
- **Four credentials, four roles.** Associate: Foundations (everyday practical use — consultants, project leads, business and technical roles), Developer: Foundations (Claude API, tool use, agent development), Architect: Foundations (designing and building agent systems), Architect: Professional (integration architecture, governance, evaluation at large-enterprise scale). Three are new; Architect: Foundations existed before.
- **Every path starts at foundation level** and advances to the professional level.
- **Validated capability, not course attendance.** Proctored, identity-verified exams designed by subject matter experts to reflect real field work.
- **Pearson delivers the program.** Exams run through Pearson Professional Assessments with identity validation before the exam begins; passing produces a digital badge through Credly by Pearson.
- **Credentials are role-matched by design** so the buyer knows what a certified person was tested on.
- **Tier standing is a different signal than certification count.** The Claude Partner Network combines certified practitioners with deployed customers and public customer references. Global Premier requires 1,000 certified practitioners, 100 customers across three regions, and 15 public customer endorsements; the next tier review is October 1.
- **Scale so far:** more than 36,000 consultants certified across 1,300+ organizations since March 2026, and more than 400,000 people trained this year through Anthropic Partner Academy.
- **Firms are committing in large cohorts** — Accenture 50,000, PwC 30,000, Capgemini/DXC/UST 20,000 each, Deloitte/KPMG 15,000 each, Cognizant/Infosys/TCS 10,000 each — alongside boutique and AI-native firms certifying whole delivery teams (Ascendion's full 8,000-person engineering team before 2027, EPAM 10,000+ architects, Wipro 10,000 within 18 months).
- **Access is through the partner network.** Preparation courses are in Anthropic Partner Academy; exams are available to Claude Partner Network members, and membership is free.
