## What is this post?
Anthropic's update to Claude for Small Business: the plugin now has 43 workflows and 27 new
integrations (Shopify, Salesforce, TikTok, Atlassian, Zoom, Xero, Gusto, Square, Stripe, Zapier and
more), extending Claude from back-office work to growing the business. It also announces a fall
schedule of free workshops, trainer-led community sessions, and partner webinars.

## When is it useful?
- A small business owner wants Claude to run a weekly brief, answer leads, write proposals, plan
  marketing, or close the month.
- An owner is weighing data-security concerns and wants to know how approvals and permissions work.
- Someone is looking for free training or a partner to help with setup.

## Key points
- Installed more than 900,000 times since the May launch; the release is built around requests
  from 1,000+ owners on the spring tour, about a third of whom wanted help growing the business.
- A sample week: `/smb-onboard`, `/monday-brief`, `/speed-to-lead`, `/proposal-builder`,
  `/social-content-engine` and `/marketing-monday`, `/close-month`, plus `/build-connector` and
  `/build-agent` for gaps. Every workflow has a fallback that works from uploaded files.
- Owner stories: Mothership Coffee Roasters, Bambi Baby, KANE, Rebel Cheese, HireEffect.
- Controls: approval mode by default, per-workflow opt-in to run on its own, payroll staged in Gusto
  for the owner to submit, existing software permissions respected, no training on business data
  by default on Team and Enterprise.
- Training: Claude SMB Tour with Tenex in 10 US cities, 150+ Approved Claude SMB Trainers running
  750+ workshops, 14 partner webinars; new SMB partners in the Claude Partner Network and community
  partnerships (Goldman Sachs 10,000 Small Businesses, IncuVersity, Echoing Green).
- Available on every paid plan; Team is recommended for businesses with more than one person.
