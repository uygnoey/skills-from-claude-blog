# Controls, plans, training, and partners

## Built for trust
Half the owners surveyed on the spring tour named data security as their biggest hesitation.
- Every workflow starts in approval mode: Claude drafts and stages, then waits for the owner's OK.
  The owner can let a workflow run on its own, one workflow at a time, and switch it back anytime.
- Some workflows stop short of the last step (for example, payroll is staged in Gusto for the
  owner to submit).
- Existing software permissions hold: if an employee cannot see something in Intuit QuickBooks or
  Google Drive, they cannot see it through Claude.
- By default, Anthropic does not train on business data on Team and Enterprise plans.

## Getting started
- Available on every paid Claude plan; Team is recommended for businesses with more than one
  person. Install, say "set me up," and connect a tool or two. An install guide and workflow list
  are on Claude Academy.
