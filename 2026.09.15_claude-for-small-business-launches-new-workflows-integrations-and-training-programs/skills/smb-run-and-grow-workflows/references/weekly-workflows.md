# A week with Claude for Small Business

The post walks through a sample owner's week. Each workflow can be started by asking in plain
language or typing the command.

| When | Workflow | Ask | Command | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sunday 8:00pm | Install and onboard | "help me get set up" | `/smb-onboard` | Tools connected, first task picked; workflows can be scheduled. |
| Monday 7:00am | Weekly brief | "give me my Monday brief" | `/monday-brief` | Cash, sales vs. prior week, pipeline moves, overdue invoices, three things that need you. Can run weekly and push to a dashboard via an artifact or website. |
| Monday 9:40pm | Inbound leads | "answer new leads as they come in" | `/speed-to-lead` | Inquiry qualified, reply drafted with two real calendar openings, logged to CRM. Standing workflow on your schedule. |
| Wednesday | Proposals | "turn this into a proposal" | `/proposal-builder` | Branded proposal priced from past jobs; out for signature after approval. Attach memo, photos, RFP, or a call transcript first. |
| Thursday | Marketing | "plan next week's content" | `/social-content-engine` | Posts in your voice with on-brand graphics, cut for each channel, staged for approval. |
| Thursday (weekly) | Marketing numbers | — | `/marketing-monday` | Sales by channel with marketing results; new reviews with replies drafted. |
| Month end | Close the books | "close September" | `/close-month` | Reconciliation against sales settlements, card spend, payroll; mismatches to the transaction; plain-English P&L; accountant packet. |
| Any gap | Missing connector | "connect my POS" | `/build-connector` | Reaches a tool through Zapier or builds a connector. |
| Any gap | Save a manual step | "make this part of the close" | `/build-agent` | A hand adjustment becomes a saved step next time. |

## No-connector fallbacks
| Workflow | Fallback |
|---|---|
| Monday brief | Upload a spreadsheet; Claude builds the brief and reports from what you share. |
| Speed to lead | Forward the inquiry; send the drafted reply yourself; leads kept in a spreadsheet CRM. |
| Proposal builder | Upload memo, photos, and a past proposal as template; send for signature yourself. |
| Social content | Claude drafts calendar, captions, design briefs; weekly page from exported sales and pasted reviews. |
| Close the month | Upload bank and processor statements; Claude closes from those. |
