# Owner stories cited in the post

| Business | Workflow | What they did | Result cited |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mothership Coffee Roasters (Las Vegas, 6 cafés + wholesale/catering) | Monday brief | Connected POS, QuickBooks, Slack, and email; Claude became the leadership team's workspace for forecasting and weekly planning. | One consolidated view of six locations; in-store margins rose to 22%, credited in part to tighter scheduling, ordering, and inventory reporting. |
| Bambi Baby (stroller and car seat retailer) | Speed to lead | A non-developer COO built a scan-to-wishlist lead capture and follow-up. | $60,000 in sales tied to it in four days; 18% of online revenue newly attributed to in-person visits two weeks into a two-store pilot. |
| KANE (creative and growth agency, Princeton) | Proposal builder | Pipeline scores fit from meeting notes, prices from past projects, drafts the proposal, sends the SOW for signature. | Weeks to minutes from scoping to SOW out for signature. |
| Rebel Cheese (plant-based cheesemaker, Austin) | Social content engine | Founder trained a voice skill on her own writing and connected email and social tools. | A teammate drafts on-brand campaigns without routing every line through the founder. |
| HireEffect (bookkeeping/payroll/HR firm, Dallas) | Close the month | Isolated a $13,000 discrepancy across years of PayPal transactions; built a governed reporting dashboard across five systems. | 16 transactions found; a two-hour monthly task cut to ten seconds. |

Spring-tour quotes also mention a 6:00am CRM-driven "daily briefing" email (Driller Design Co.)
and proposals that brought in $20,000 in six weeks (Modern Classical Chefs).
