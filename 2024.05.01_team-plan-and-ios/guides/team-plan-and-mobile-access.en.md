# Team plan and mobile access

A short survey of the two product changes announced in the source post, and what each one is for.

## What was announced

**The Claude Team plan** — a paid workspace plan aimed at teams rather than individuals.

**The Claude iOS app** — a free mobile app, available across plans.

## The Team plan

| Aspect | What the post states |
|---|---|
| Usage | Increased usage compared with individual plans |
| Models | Access to the Claude 3 model family — Opus, Sonnet, and Haiku |
| Context window | 200K |
| Administration | Admin tools for managing the workspace |
| Feature baseline | Everything included in Claude Pro |
| Price | $30 per user per month |
| Minimum | 5 seats |

### When it fits

- A team needs more per-user usage than an individual plan provides.
- Someone has to administer membership and billing centrally rather than per person.
- The work benefits from choosing between models in the Claude 3 family by task — a larger model for hard reasoning, a smaller one for volume.
- Long-context work is routine enough that the 200K window matters.

### When it does not

- A single person working alone: the 5-seat minimum makes the per-person economics worse than an individual plan.
- A team that only needs occasional access and is not usage-constrained.

## The iOS app

| Aspect | What the post states |
|---|---|
| Price | Free, on all plans |
| Chat sync | Conversations sync across devices |
| Vision | Supports photos and files |

### What it changes in practice

The two capabilities worth planning around are sync and vision.

**Sync** means mobile is a continuation surface rather than a separate one. A conversation started at a desk can be picked up in transit and finished later at the desk — useful for review and follow-up rather than for long composition.

**Vision on a phone** means the camera becomes an input path. Photographing a document, a whiteboard, a screen, or a physical object is often faster than transferring a file to a computer first.

## Practical notes

- Confirm current pricing, seat minimums, model availability, and feature sets before making a purchasing decision. The figures above are as stated at the time of the announcement and product details change.
- Anything not stated in the source post — regional availability, procurement terms, data handling specifics — should be checked against current official documentation rather than inferred from this summary.
