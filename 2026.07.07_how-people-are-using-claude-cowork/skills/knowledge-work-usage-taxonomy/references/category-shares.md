# Category shares (May 11–31, 2026 sample)

Published shares of 1.2 million sampled, anonymized, aggregated Claude Cowork
sessions from more than 600,000 organizations, classified by an automated system
into a taxonomy of 20 categories of work.

Every figure is a **share of sampled sessions**, not an absolute volume. See
[methodology-and-limits.md](methodology-and-limits.md) before quoting any of them.

## Named categories

| Category | Share | What it covers (as described in the post) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business process and operations | 33.4% | Pulling scattered updates into a single report, building onboarding checklists, reconciling spreadsheets |
| Content creation and copywriting | 16.4% | Synthesis-intensive business communications — drafts, slide decks, posts, proposals |
| Software development | 8.7% | — |
| DevOps and infrastructure | 7% | — |
| Research and intelligence | 6.4% | Research and intelligence work |
| Data analysis and business intelligence | 5.8% | — |
| Document processing and extraction | 4.1% | — |
| Sales and revenue operations | 4% | — |
| Personal assistance | 3.8% | — |
| Education | 2.4% | — |
| Meeting and conversation intelligence | 1.8% | — |
| Legal and compliance | 1.3% | — |
| Customer support | 0.8% | — |

The taxonomy has 20 categories; the post names the ones above. Categories not
named individually each fall below the thresholds discussed in the post.

## Reading the table

- **The gap matters more than the top number.** Business process and operations
  (33.4%) is more than double content creation and copywriting (16.4%). Every
  other category is under 9%.
- **The top two together are roughly half of all usage.** That half is the post's
  central claim: it is "the work around the work" — tasks that are part of a broad
  swath of jobs, but are rarely a person's core responsibility.
- **Personal, non-work use is present.** Personal assistance, hobbies, and
  companionship-style conversations together account for roughly 5% of sessions.

## Categories that do not exist

There are no standalone categories for **marketing**, **finance**, or **HR**. Those
functions are best represented by "business process and operations", which is
likely part of why that category occupies a third of all usage. Any conclusion that
depends on separating those three functions cannot be drawn from this taxonomy.
