## What is this post?
Anthropic's announcement of Claude for Financial Advisors: new connectors to custodians, portfolio
platforms, CRMs, planning, estate, and meeting-capture tools, plus a plugin of eight skills built
around an advisor's day (meeting prep, follow-up, portfolio and estate reviews, compliance).

## When is it useful?
- An advisor or RIA wants Claude to prepare meeting briefs and follow-up from connected systems.
- A firm is deciding which connectors to enable and how approvals should work.
- Compliance staff want to know what the compliance and AI policy skill does and does not decide.

## Key points
- New connectors: Addepar, BlackRock, Charles Schwab, Envestnet, iCapital, Orion, SS&C Black
  Diamond, Wealthbox, Wealth.com, Vanguard, Zocks — joining Microsoft 365, Salesforce, DocuSign,
  Box, FactSet, S&P Global, Morningstar and others.
- Eight skills: advisor onboarding, alternative investments brief, compliance and AI policy, estate
  and tax brief, portfolio rebalance review, post-meeting notes and follow-up, pre-meeting prep,
  prospect intake. Firms can use them as-is or adapt them to their house style.
- Claude drafts and stages CRM updates and client communications for approval; investment
  recommendations, client communications, and compliance determinations stay with humans.
- The compliance skill screens client-facing language against the SEC Marketing Rule and helps
  document AI use.
- Motivation: Kitces research finds practices spend about a sixth of their time in client meetings.
- Install from Cowork's plugin browser; Enterprise is recommended for RIAs because of audit logs.
