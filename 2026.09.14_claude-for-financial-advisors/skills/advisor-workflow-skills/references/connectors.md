# Connectors announced for financial advisors

| Partner | What it brings into Claude |
|---|---|
| Addepar | Governed access to portfolio data, analytics, and workflows across public and private markets. |
| BlackRock | Portfolio construction expertise, model portfolios, and institutional analytics via Advisor Center. BlackRock is also launching its own Advisor Center plugin. |
| Charles Schwab | Schwab Advisor Services custodial data: balances, positions, transactions, cost basis, alerts, money-movement status. |
| Envestnet | Natural-language summaries of Tamarac accounts and households, with a MoneyGuide financial plan snapshot. |
| iCapital | Alternative holdings: NAV, commitments, unfunded capital, recent capital activity, performance. |
| Orion | Portfolio reporting and Redtail CRM insights for household reviews, opportunity analysis, meeting prep, and follow-up. |
| SS&C Black Diamond | Portfolio, performance, holdings, and rebalancing data; spot clients out of tolerance, surface tax-loss harvesting opportunities, generate reports, tasks, and rebalance sessions with approval. |
| Wealthbox | Client records and meeting history for onboarding, prep, and follow-up. |
| Wealth.com | Structured estate and tax picture: trust and will summaries, tax return insights, full balance sheet. |
| Vanguard | Model portfolios and advisor investment solutions; research, portfolio construction, and asset allocation expertise. |
| Zocks | AI meeting assistant data: client profiles, goals, life events, commitments. |

Already available alongside these: Microsoft 365, Salesforce, DocuSign, Box, FactSet, S&P Global,
Morningstar, and more. S&P Global and LSEG also have their own plugins.

The plugin's guided setup lets advisors choose which of BlackRock, Charles Schwab, Addepar,
Envestnet, iCapital, Orion, Wealthbox, Wealth.com, and Zocks to connect.
