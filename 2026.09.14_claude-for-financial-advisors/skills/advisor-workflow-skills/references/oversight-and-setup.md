# Oversight rules and setup

## Advisor stays in control
- Claude prepares briefs, summaries, and draft analyses for advisor review.
- Administrative actions (CRM updates, draft client communications) are staged for review and
  approval; approval is required on critical tasks.
- Investment recommendations, client communications, compliance determinations, and other
  regulated activities remain subject to human review and approval.
- The compliance skill screens client-facing language against the SEC Marketing Rule to flag
  potential issues, helps document review activity within existing governance and recordkeeping,
  and includes a self-guided workflow for documenting AI use under SEC rules.
- Workflow activity can be documented to help firms keep records and oversight processes.

## Why it matters (figures cited in the post)
- Kitces research: a typical advisory practice spends only about a sixth of its time in client
  meetings.
- EBRI 2026 Retirement Confidence Survey: over four in ten American workers do not know who to go
  to for good financial or retirement planning advice.

## Getting started
1. Plan: Enterprise is recommended for registered investment advisers because it includes audit
   logs that support recordkeeping.
2. With an Enterprise license: open Cowork, find "Claude for Financial Advisors" in the plugin
   browser (or the marketplace), install, and follow guided setup to connect tools.
3. Without one: request a license through Anthropic's form. Requests before the end of September
   2026 received a one-time usage credit (time-limited offer at publication).
