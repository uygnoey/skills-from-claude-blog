# Claude for Financial Advisors — skills catalog

Each skill supports one moment in an advisor's day and routes to tools the firm already uses.
Firms can adopt them as-is from Anthropic's financial advisors skill repository or adapt them.

| Skill | What it does | Typical sources |
|---|---|---|
| Advisor onboarding | Connects the firm's tools and runs the first meeting prep automatically, so a new advisor is productive on day one instead of week three. | All connected tools |
| Alternative investments brief | Pulls a household's alternatives into a meeting-ready summary shown alongside the rest of the portfolio, instead of reconciling separate statements by hand. | iCapital, Addepar |
| Compliance and AI policy | Highlights content that may warrant extra compliance review based on user-configured criteria; helps document review workflows; supports AI-governance documentation. Screens client-facing language against the SEC Marketing Rule and includes a self-guided AI-policy workflow. | Draft content, firm criteria |
| Estate and tax brief | Checks the estate plan against actual account titling and beneficiaries, looks back at the prior year's taxes, and can create CRM follow-up tasks so mismatches surface for review. | Wealth.com, custodian, CRM |
| Portfolio rebalance review | Flags drift and concentrated positions against the target allocation and drafts a first explanation the advisor would otherwise write from scratch. | SS&C Black Diamond, Orion, Addepar, custodian |
| Post-meeting notes and follow-up | Turns a transcript into a client summary, a recap email, and CRM tasks in one pass. | Zocks, CRM (Wealthbox, Redtail via Orion, Salesforce) |
| Pre-meeting prep | Consolidates holdings, recent account activity, and open items from prior meetings into one brief for the advisor to review. | Custodian, portfolio reporting, CRM, meeting capture |
| Prospect intake | Organizes what a prospect shares into a summary, an analyst handoff, and a what-to-expect memo. | Prospect documents, CRM |

Together the skills gather information, summarize it, and draft client communications.
Investment recommendations, client communications, compliance determinations, and other
regulated activities remain subject to human review and approval.
