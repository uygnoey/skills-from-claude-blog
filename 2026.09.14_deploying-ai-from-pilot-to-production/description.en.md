## What is this post?
A perspectives article (September 14, 2026) introducing a practical blueprint, written by Anthropic with
Accenture, for CIOs and technical leaders moving AI programs from pilot into production. The full guide
is a linked PDF.

## When is it useful?
- When scoping a pilot so its results predict production performance.
- When preparing a go/no-go decision or assigning owners for AI costs and outcomes.
- When designing how much human review each kind of AI output needs.

## Key points
- Pilots are designed to succeed (handpicked teams, protected budgets, curated data), so they are
  unrepresentative of production. Only 23% of C-suite leaders report sustained, enterprise-wide impact.
- 42% of organizations share AI cost and outcome accountability between IT and finance with no single
  owner; name one owner with decision rights, escalation authority, and executive backing.
- Seven considerations in order: pre-pilot (strategic objectives/ROI/ownership, data readiness and
  integration, infrastructure and architecture, security/compliance/trust), pilot to production
  (organizational readiness), in production (governance and risk, scale and evolution).
- Each ends with "work out" questions for the cross-functional team and "assign" ownership decisions.
- Four-part use-case definition (user, task, output, measurable quality threshold) and a lightweight
  TCO model built before the pilot and revisited quarterly.
- Four-tier oversight model - automated, sampled, reviewed, advisory - with example tasks and review
  cadence; audit checkpoints, define incident response, monitor continuously.
- Scale from copilots to end-to-end automation, starting simple and understanding error modes first.
