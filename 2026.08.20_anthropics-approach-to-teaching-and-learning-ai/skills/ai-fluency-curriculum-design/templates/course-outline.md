# Course outline template

A scaffold for one unit of an AI-fluency course. Built so that a unit cannot be
finished without the three active-learning elements the source names: a practice
exercise, a reflection prompt, and room to experiment.

Copy per unit. Delete the guidance in italics.

## Unit checklist before publishing

- [ ] Title names a problem, not a feature
- [ ] Learner makes an explicit delegation decision
- [ ] Mechanics section is not the longest section
- [ ] Verification is calibrated to stakes, not stated as an absolute rule
- [ ] Skill preservation and disclosure both appear
- [ ] All three active-learning elements present and on the learner's own work
- [ ] At least one durable framing stated
- [ ] Nothing in the unit will be wrong after the next model release — anything
      that would be belongs in the continuing ("ever-boarding") track instead
