# Conversation outcomes

Every buying-agent conversation ends one of three ways (from the source article):

| Outcome | When | What the agent does |
|---|---|---|
| **Purchase** | The buyer is ready and self-serve covers the need | Sends the buyer straight to checkout with the recommended plan and seat count |
| **Hand-off** | Larger or more complex deals | Passes the buyer to a rep **with the full conversation details** and the reason for escalating |
| **Quick answer** | The buyer only needed information | Answers (pricing, security, data, plan fit) and ends |

## Hand-off payload (what the rep should start with)
The source says a rep "starts the conversation already knowing what the customer needs, what they've
been told, and how we can best help them." So include:
- What the customer needs / is trying to solve
- Current usage context
- What the agent already told them (answers, recommendation)
- Why the agent escalated (feeds the escalation feedback loop)

## Entry points
Contact Sales page, Pricing page, inside the product, and emails. The experience is opt-in: the
customer chooses agent or rep at the start.
