## What is this post?
An announcement from October 28, 2026. Claude for Legal now includes ready-to-use artifacts: interactive
tools such as trackers and dashboards that legal teams can adapt with their own data and connectors and
share across the business. Since Claude for Legal launched in May, legal teams most often asked for simpler
setup and easier ways to work with colleagues.

## When is it useful?
- A legal team needs to review a stack of contracts, check citations, compare a rule across jurisdictions,
  track open matters, or watch upcoming regulations, and wants a reusable tool.
- Business colleagues should send legal requests through one place in Claude.
- You need to share legal work with colleagues, clients, or partners while keeping privacy and ethical walls.
- You work in legal aid or public interest law and want a starting tool for access-to-justice work.

## Key points
- The working pattern: the legal professional brings documents and sources, Claude does the first pass, and
  a lawyer applies judgment. Each artifact opens with an example, and each answer links to its source.
- Gallery artifacts: Cite check (mismatched quotes, wrong page numbers, unsupported claims), Jurisdiction
  survey matrix (one topic across states or countries, linked to the law), Matter board (owner, next step,
  next deadline), Playbook deviation board (clauses that differ from standard terms, sent to the exceptions
  approver), Regulatory horizon scan (upcoming rules on a timeline, ranked by impact), and Tabular review
  (chosen terms across contracts, linked to the exact language).
- Teams can ask Claude to tailor artifacts and use connectors for playbooks and research sources instead of
  manual uploads. More than 60 legal connectors have been added, including Bloomberg Law, Filevine, Ivo,
  Legora, and Patlytics.
- Artifacts can be shared inside and outside the organization with settings that ensure privacy and support
  ethical walls between teams.
- Partner artifacts: AI Governance Benchmark (Goodwin), Harvey Intake (Harvey), Obligations Checker (Foley &
  Lardner), and Subpoena Response Tool (PwC). More are coming from Legora and Orrick.
- Nine access-to-justice artifacts, five built by nonprofits supported with Claude credits: Criminal Charge
  Reality Check (Zealous, New York), Immigration Papers Reader (Mobile Pathways), Rent Ledger Reviewer and
  Record-Sealing Screener (Rising for Justice, D.C.), and Record Clearance (The Access Project).
- Anthropic is funding public interest groups and legal education programs, and is launching a Legal
  Ambassadors Program (waitlist open). Rollouts can be planned with sales or implementation partners such as
  Accenture, Deloitte, KPMG, and PwC.
