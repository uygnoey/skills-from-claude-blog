# Connectors and rollout

## Legal connectors
- Since Claude for Legal launched in May, more than 60 legal connectors have been added.
- Named in the post: Bloomberg Law, Filevine, Ivo, Legora, Patlytics.
- Full list: the legal directory at `https://claude.ai/directory/legal`.
- Why it matters: connectors bring playbooks and research sources into artifacts, so nobody has to upload
  documents manually.

Examples of how partners describe their connections (paraphrased; see the original for exact wording):
- **Ivo:** contracts already kept in Ivo become shareable artifacts, such as a renewal calendar tracking
  every notice date across a portfolio, with no manual uploads. Answers are backed by contract language and
  Ivo stays the system of record.
- **Legora:** answers to legal questions inside enterprise AI workflows, with links back to Legora for
  source review, citations, and deeper work.
- **Filevine (and LOIS):** customers act directly on their case files from wherever they work.
- **Docusign:** legal teams and cross-functional stakeholders create, review, and move agreements forward
  in Claude.
- **Ironclad:** contracting intelligence brought into the platforms business teams already use.
