# Partner and access-to-justice artifacts

## Built by legal tech companies and law firms
Each is in the gallery for legal professionals to discover, test, and adapt.

| Artifact | Built by | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| AI Governance Benchmark | Goodwin | Benchmarks a company's approach to AI governance to surface potential gaps |
| Harvey Intake | Harvey | One place in Claude for business users to send legal questions and requests; answers routine ones from Legal's own guidance in Harvey and routes the rest to a lawyer, whose reply comes back in Claude |
| Obligations Checker | Foley & Lardner | Reviews agreements for both parties' obligations; helps validate expectations before closing and track them after closing |
| Subpoena Response Tool | PwC | Turns the team's precedents, standard objections, and templates into automated, audit-ready first drafts |

The post says more artifacts are coming from Legora and Orrick, and invites legal tech companies, law
firms, and legal aid organizations to create and share their own.

## Access to justice
The gallery includes nine access-to-justice artifacts. Five were built by nonprofit and public interest
organizations supported with Claude credits; the post names these five:

| Artifact | Built by | What it does | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| Criminal Charge Reality Check | Zealous | Takes a criminal charge and shows defense teams the sentencing range, public data on how such cases usually end, and standard playbooks for release | New York only |
| Immigration Papers Reader | Mobile Pathways | Turns a client's immigration paperwork into a plain-language first read of key dates and missing documents | Not stated |
| Rent Ledger Reviewer | Rising for Justice | Helps attorneys find errors in a landlord's rent ledger when preparing an eviction case | D.C. law |
| Record-Sealing Screener | Rising for Justice | Helps attorneys assess whether a client's criminal record is eligible for sealing | D.C. law |
| Record Clearance | The Access Project | Helps legal aid teams work out whether a client's record can be cleared under their state's rules | State rules |

The other four access-to-justice artifacts are not named in the post.

## Related programs
- **Oxford Justice Innovators Programme:** a 12-month program with the Oxford Institute of Technology and
  Justice and Microsoft, supporting up to 10 innovators expanding access to justice.
- **Funding:** Anthropic is funding public interest groups building access-to-justice artifacts and legal
  education programs that teach future lawyers to build with Claude.
- **Legal Ambassadors Program:** a community for legal professionals who build with Claude to share work and
  learn from each other. A waitlist is open (link in the original post).
