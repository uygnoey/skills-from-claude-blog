# Legal gallery: core artifacts

These are the six task artifacts the post lists. For each one, the legal professional brings the documents
and sources, Claude does the first pass, and a lawyer reviews. Every output links to its source.

| Artifact | What you bring | What it produces |
|---|---|---|
| Cite check | A legal document and the sources it cites | Flags quotes that don't match the source, wrong page numbers, and sources that don't support the claim |
| Jurisdiction survey matrix | A legal topic (the post's example: data breach notice rules) and the states or countries to compare | One table comparing the topic across jurisdictions, with links to the underlying law |
| Matter board | Engagement letters, emails, or spreadsheets | One board of open matters showing the owner, next step, and next deadline |
| Playbook deviation board | Contracts and your company's standard terms | Each clause that differs from the standard is flagged and sent to the person who approves exceptions |
| Regulatory horizon scan | Your company's business and where it operates | Upcoming regulations on a timeline, ranked by impact, with links to the sources |
| Tabular review | A set of contracts and the terms you ask about (the post's examples: severance, non-competes) | A table of those terms across the contracts, with links to the exact language |

## What the lawyer checks
The post frames every artifact as a first pass followed by a lawyer's judgment. Practical checks that
follow from the outputs above:

- **Cite check:** confirm each flag against the cited page before changing the document.
- **Jurisdiction survey matrix:** open the linked law for any jurisdiction the advice depends on.
- **Matter board:** confirm owners and deadlines with the people named.
- **Playbook deviation board:** the approver decides whether to accept each exception.
- **Regulatory horizon scan:** confirm the impact ranking against the business's actual footprint.
- **Tabular review:** read the linked language for any term that drives a decision.

## Common properties
- Each artifact opens with an example so you can see how the work comes together first.
- Artifacts can be tailored to the team by asking Claude.
- Connectors can feed playbooks and research sources in, so documents don't need manual upload.
- Artifacts can be shared inside and outside the organization with privacy and ethical-wall settings.
