# Sharing checklist

The post says artifacts can be shared with colleagues across the business, or with clients and partners,
using sharing settings that ensure privacy and support ethical walls between teams. Use this sheet before
sharing. Exact setting names are in the product; see the original post and the Claude for Legal page.

## Guardrails
- [ ] Sharing settings limit access to the audience above.
- [ ] Any ethical wall between teams is respected: people on the wrong side of the wall cannot open it.
- [ ] Content from other matters or clients is not exposed through connected sources.
- [ ] Outputs a reader will rely on have been reviewed by a lawyer.
- [ ] Source links work for the audience, or the audience knows they may not.
