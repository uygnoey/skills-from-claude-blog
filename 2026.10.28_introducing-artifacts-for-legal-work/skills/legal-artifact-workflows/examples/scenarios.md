# Scenarios and customer voices

## Scenarios from the post
- **Obligations before and after closing.** A deal team runs Foley & Lardner's Obligations Checker on the
  agreements to see both parties' obligations, validate expectations before closing, and track them after.
- **Subpoena responses.** A team feeds its precedents, standard objections, and templates into PwC's
  Subpoena Response Tool and gets audit-ready first drafts, leaving lawyers the complex issues.
- **AI governance gaps.** A company works through Goodwin's AI Governance Benchmark to see where its
  approach has gaps and which areas to address first.
- **Renewal calendar.** A team turns contracts kept in Ivo into a shared renewal calendar that tracks every
  notice date across the portfolio.
- **Record clearance.** A legal aid team uses The Access Project's Record Clearance artifact to screen for
  eligibility, obtain the record, see how state law applies, and find local filing guidance.
- **Eviction defense in D.C.** An attorney uses Rising for Justice's Rent Ledger Reviewer to find errors in
  a landlord's rent ledger, and the Record-Sealing Screener to assess sealing eligibility.
- **Criminal defense in New York.** A defense team enters a charge into Zealous's Criminal Charge Reality
  Check to see the sentencing range, how such cases usually end, and release playbooks, so more time goes
  to the person.
- **Immigration paperwork.** A team uses Mobile Pathways' Immigration Papers Reader for a plain-language
  first read of key dates and missing documents.
