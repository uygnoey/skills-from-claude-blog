## What is this post?
An announcement that Anthropic is expanding the Claude Startups program to give founders access to
its models, tools, and people from the start. An update on 2026-10-13 says the cohort is full and new
applications are not being accepted; check the program page for current status.

## When is it useful?
- When a founder wants to know whether they qualify for Claude startup credits.
- When planning how to use Team seats, API credit, and partner discounts.

## Key points
- Eligibility: startups founded in the last five years or funded in the last two.
- Claude credits worth up to $7,000: a free year of Claude Team (up to five Premium seats, for
  companies new to Team) and a $1,000 one-time API credit once approved.
- Claude Startup Stack: discounts and credits worth up to $45,000 on partner tools for sales, design,
  data engineering, and other functions.
- Also: virtual office hours with the Applied AI team, help getting listed on the Claude Marketplace,
  and events (Founder House, hackathons, meetups) plus the Startup Newsletter.
- Caveats: the $7,000 figure assumes five Premium seats at $100/seat/month billed annually; credits
  follow the Supplemental Credit Terms; Stack values use September 2026 list prices.
- Apply at claude.com/programs/startups.
