# Claude Startups program — benefits reference

As announced 2026-10-06. Update 2026-10-13: the cohort was full and new applications were not being
accepted. Check claude.com/programs/startups for current status.

## Eligibility
- Founded in the last five years, **or**
- Funded in the last two years.

## Benefits

| Benefit | Detail |
|---|---|
| Claude credits (up to $7,000) | Free year of Claude Team, up to five Premium seats (companies new to Team) + $1,000 one-time API credit once approved |
| Claude Startup Stack | Discounts and credits worth up to $45,000 on partner tools (sales, design, data engineering, other business functions) |
| Office hours | Virtual sessions with Anthropic's Applied AI team |
| Claude Marketplace | Help getting a listing where Claude users find connectors and plugins |
| Community | Founder House, hackathons, meetups (limited space), Startup Newsletter |

## Footnotes from the post
- $7,000 assumes five Premium seats at $100 per seat per month, billed annually.
- Credits are subject to Anthropic's Supplemental Credit Terms.
- Stack values are based on partner list prices as of September 2026; partner offers are subject to
  their own terms.

## Founder checklist
- [ ] Program page shows applications are open
- [ ] Founding date within five years, or last funding within two years
- [ ] Not already on Claude Team (for the free Team year)
- [ ] Seat owners chosen (up to five)
- [ ] First API project chosen for the $1,000 credit
- [ ] Stack offers that match real needs shortlisted
- [ ] Questions prepared for office hours
