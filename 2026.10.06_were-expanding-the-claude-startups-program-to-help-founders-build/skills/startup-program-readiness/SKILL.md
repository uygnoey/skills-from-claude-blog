## Purpose
Anthropic expanded the Claude Startups program (announced 2026-10-06) to give founders access to its
models, tools, and people from day one. The post's framing: building software is easier than ever,
but standing out and covering startup costs are still hard. This skill turns the announcement into an
eligibility check and a plan for using the benefits.

## When to use
- A founder asks whether they qualify for Claude startup credits.
- A founder wants to plan how to use Team seats, API credit, and partner discounts.

## Instructions

1. **Check current status first.** An update dated 2026-10-13 says the cohort was full and new
   applications were paused. Send the founder to claude.com/programs/startups for the current status
   before going further. Do not promise availability.

2. **Check eligibility.** The startup must be founded in the last five years **or** funded in the
   last two. Ask for founding date and most recent funding date; if neither fits, say so plainly.

3. **Map the benefits** (details in [references/program-benefits.md](references/program-benefits.md)):
   - Claude credits worth up to $7,000:
     - A free year of Claude Team, up to five Premium seats — only for companies new to Team.
     - A $1,000 one-time API credit once approved.
   - Claude Startup Stack: discounts and credits worth up to $45,000 on partner tools for sales,
     design, data engineering, and other functions.
   - Virtual office hours with Anthropic's Applied AI team.
   - Help getting listed on the Claude Marketplace (where users find connectors and plugins).
   - Events (Founder House, hackathons, meetups; limited space) and the Startup Newsletter.

4. **Flag the caveats.**
   - The $7,000 figure assumes five Premium seats at $100/seat/month billed annually.
   - Credits are subject to Anthropic's Supplemental Credit Terms.
   - Stack values use partner list prices as of September 2026; partner offers have their own terms.
   - Existing Team customers do not get the free Team year ("new to Team").

5. **Make a usage plan.** Help the founder decide who gets the Team seats, what to build with the API
   credit, which Stack offers they actually need, what to bring to office hours, and whether a
   Marketplace listing (connector or plugin) fits their product. Keep this to the founder's real
   needs; do not invent offers or partner names.

6. **Point to the application.** Apply at claude.com/programs/startups (when open).

## Examples
- Founder: "We incorporated in 2019 and raised a seed round in March 2026. Do we qualify?"
  → Founded more than five years ago, but funded within the last two years → eligible on the funding
  criterion. Remind them to check whether applications are open.
- Founder: "We already pay for Claude Team. What do we get?"
  → The free Team year is for companies new to Team; the API credit, Stack offers, office hours,
  Marketplace help, and events may still apply — confirm on the program page.
