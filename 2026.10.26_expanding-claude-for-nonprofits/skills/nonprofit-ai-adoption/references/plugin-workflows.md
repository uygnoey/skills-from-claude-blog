# Claude for Nonprofits plugin - workflow catalog

Source: https://claude.com/resources/articles/expanding-claude-for-nonprofits
The post describes these capabilities; it does not publish prompts or code. Phrasing below summarizes the post.

## 1. Extend your team
| Area | What the plugin helps with |
|---|---|
| Marketing | Campaign planning sized to team bandwidth; drafting in the organization's voice; content review |
| Hiring and people | Job posts with interview guides and scoring rubrics; 90-day onboarding plans; answering staff questions from the employee handbook |
| Board and leadership | Identifying board skill gaps; outreach drafts for candidates; board orientation packets; meeting deck planning |

## 2. Set up Claude responsibly
| Workflow | What it does |
|---|---|
| Map your organization | Identifies where each record type lives (donor data, grant files, finances, policies). With memory on, the map is saved per person and used by other skills |
| Check your AI policy | Staff ask whether a use is allowed; Claude answers from the organization's own policy. No policy yet: Claude Academy tutorial |

## 3. Fund your mission
| Workflow | What it does |
|---|---|
| Find funders and grants | Research donors, foundations, and companies; surface open grants with cited sources |
| Decide what to pursue | Forward an RFP to get an eligibility and competitiveness breakdown |
| Write proposals and reports | Turn an RFP into a requirements list and first draft, with cited report figures |
| Keep relationships warm | Pre-call briefs; post-call write-ups and thank-you notes; donor updates in the organization's voice; stewardship plans |
