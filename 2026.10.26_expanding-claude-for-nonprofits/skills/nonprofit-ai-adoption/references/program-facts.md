# Claude for Nonprofits - program facts (as of October 26, 2026)

Source: https://claude.com/resources/articles/expanding-claude-for-nonprofits

## Timeline
- **December (prior year):** Claude for Nonprofits launched - discounts up to 75%, connectors for
  Blackbaud, Candid, and Benevity, and a free AI Fluency course built with GivingTuesday.
- **September 2026:** about 90% discount for verified nonprofits in low- and middle-income countries;
  standard Team seats become $3/user/month.
- **October 26, 2026:** Claude for Nonprofits plugin released; Claude Corps to be extended through a
  partnership with Taproot Foundation.

## Plans
- Nonprofit plans include the same models as standard Team and Enterprise plans.
- Team plan minimum is two people.
- Claude Code is included at no additional cost.
- The plugin installs automatically at no extra cost for nonprofits on Team and Enterprise plans.

## Adoption
- More than 25,000 nonprofits use Claude; over 75% have fewer than 10 seats.

## Barriers the post addresses
1. **Lack of AI skills**
   - Claude Corps: 1,000 early-career fellows in at least 400 U.S. nonprofits.
   - Planned Taproot Foundation partnership: pro bono consultants for short projects.
   - Named adoption partners: Accenture, BCG, Bridgespan Group, Civis Analytics, Deloitte, fusionSpan,
     Idealist Consulting, Myridius, SapienAI, Slalom, v4c.ai, Vera Solutions.
2. **Lack of suitable tools**
   - Skills built with the Design Partner Circle (about 30 organizations of varying size and geography).

## Webinars
- *Admin's Guide to Data Handling and AI Use Policy* - November 10

## Security and data
- Admins control user access and which connectors are available.
- No training on customer data by default.
- Security and compliance documents: Anthropic Trust Center.
- Claude Academy tutorial covers data handling and drafting an AI use policy.

## Getting started
- Ask Claude to map where your organization keeps its information.
- Skill examples and tutorials: Claude Academy nonprofit collection.
- Not on Claude yet: see the plans and pricing page.
- Feedback form for future releases (linked from the post).
