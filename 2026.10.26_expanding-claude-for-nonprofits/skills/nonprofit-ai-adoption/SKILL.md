## Purpose
On October 26, 2026 Anthropic released the Claude for Nonprofits plugin: skills and connectors built with
about 30 nonprofits (the Design Partner Circle). It installs automatically, at no extra cost, for nonprofits
on Team and Enterprise plans. This skill turns the announcement into a working order of operations.
Program facts are in [references/program-facts.md](references/program-facts.md); the workflow catalog is in
[references/plugin-workflows.md](references/plugin-workflows.md).

## When to use
- A nonprofit has just enabled Claude (or the plugin arrived automatically) and staff ask where to start.
- An admin must decide which connectors to allow and how to communicate data handling and AI policy.
- A development/fundraising team wants help finding funders, judging an RFP, or drafting a proposal or report.
- Operations staff need help with marketing, hiring, onboarding, or board work.

## Instructions
1. **Admin first: access and data.** Admins control which users have access and which connectors are
   available. Anthropic does not train models on customer data by default; security and compliance
   documents are in the Anthropic Trust Center. If the organization has no AI use policy yet, use the
   Claude Academy tutorial on data handling and drafting an AI use policy.
2. **Map your organization (start here).** The post's recommended first step: ask Claude to map where the
   organization keeps its information - donor data, grant files, finances, policies. With memory on, the
   map is saved per person and the other skills reuse it. Use
   [templates/organization-map.md](templates/organization-map.md) to capture the result.
3. **Check uses against your AI policy.** Staff can ask whether a given use is allowed; Claude answers from
   the organization's own policy rather than a generic rule.
4. **Pick the workflow by focus area** (details in
   [references/plugin-workflows.md](references/plugin-workflows.md)):
   - *Extend your team* - marketing, hiring and people, board and leadership.
   - *Set up Claude responsibly* - organization map, AI policy check.
   - *Fund your mission* - find funders and grants, decide what to pursue, write proposals and reports,
     keep relationships warm.
5. **For an RFP, decide before you write.** Forward the RFP and ask for an eligibility and
   competitiveness breakdown; only then turn it into a requirements list and first draft. Keep the cited
   sources and cited report figures Claude provides, and verify them before submission. A worksheet is in
   [templates/rfp-go-no-go.md](templates/rfp-go-no-go.md).
6. **Build skills in people, not just tools.** Point staff to the webinars and Claude Academy's nonprofit
   collection; consider Claude Corps fellows or pro bono consultants (planned Taproot Foundation
   partnership) and the named adoption partners in the program facts.
7. **Send feedback** through the form Anthropic linked from the post to shape future releases.

## Examples
**Example 1 - day one for a small team**
"Map where we keep donor records, grant files, finances, and policies." Claude produces the map; with
memory on, it is saved and later used by the proposal and donor-update skills.

**Example 2 - policy question**
A staff member asks: "Can I paste a donor's giving history into Claude to draft a thank-you note?" Claude
answers based on the organization's AI use policy and connector settings, not a generic rule.

**Example 3 - RFP triage**
The grants lead forwards a foundation RFP. Claude returns an eligibility and competitiveness breakdown; the
team decides to pursue, and Claude converts the RFP into a requirements list and a first draft with cited
figures from past reports.

**Example 4 - stewardship cycle**
Before a major-donor call: a pre-call brief. After: a write-up and thank-you note, then a donor update in
the organization's voice and a stewardship plan.
