## What is this post?
An announcement from October 26, 2026: Anthropic released the Claude for Nonprofits plugin - skills and
connectors built with about 30 nonprofits - and plans to extend its Claude Corps fellowship through a
partnership with Taproot Foundation.

## When is it useful?
- You work at a nonprofit on a Team or Enterprise plan and want to know where to start with Claude.
- You are an admin deciding on connectors, data handling, and an AI use policy.
- Your development team wants help with funder research, RFP decisions, proposals, reports, and stewardship.

## Key points
- Background: since launch, discounts up to 75%, connectors for Blackbaud, Candid, and Benevity, and a free
  AI Fluency course with GivingTuesday. More than 25,000 nonprofits use Claude (over 75% have fewer than
  10 seats). Since September, verified nonprofits in low- and middle-income countries get about 90% off
  ($3/user/month Team seats). Same models as standard plans, Team minimum of two people, Claude Code
  included at no extra cost.
- The plugin installs automatically at no extra cost and has three focus areas: extend your team
  (marketing, hiring and people, board and leadership), set up Claude responsibly (map your organization,
  check your AI policy), and fund your mission (find funders and grants, decide what to pursue, write
  proposals and reports, keep relationships warm).
- Skills gap: Claude Corps places 1,000 early-career fellows in at least 400 U.S. nonprofits; Taproot would
  match nonprofits with pro bono consultants; a list of adoption partners is named.
- Security: admins control access and connectors; no training on customer data by default; documents in the
  Anthropic Trust Center. Webinars on November 10 (admins) and November 12 (staff).
- First step: ask Claude to map where your organization keeps its information.
