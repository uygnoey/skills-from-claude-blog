## What is this post?
An article introducing Anthropic's guide for revenue leaders rolling out Claude across a sales
organization (linked PDF). It covers a maturity model, setup decisions before a pilot, a three-phase
rollout, ROI measurement, common pitfalls, and getting-started checklists.

## When is it useful?
- When a revenue leader or RevOps owner is planning an org-wide Claude program.
- When a pilot is running and someone must decide whether and how to scale.
- When building an ROI readout or next year's budget case.

## Key points
- Returns grow with the access and trust extended to Claude; whole-team adoption compounds beyond
  individual time savings.
- Before the pilot: RevOps as owner, connected systems, IT and security involved early, one activity
  plus one revenue metric with a baseline, spend limits by org/group/user, two or three pilot teams.
- The Sales plugin offers `/call-summary`, `/forecast`, `/pipeline-review` and skills for account
  research, call prep, daily briefing, outreach, competitive intelligence, and sales assets.
- Three phases (setup, pilot, scale in waves) with one champion per 25-50 users.
- ROI: compare pilot vs non-pilot teams in the same quarter; group returns as efficiency, expansion,
  and new capabilities; don't build the case on hours saved alone.
- Pitfalls: pilot with no end date, seats scaled without champions, spend ignored until the invoice.
- Cited results: Cox (7x first-year return, −86% lead-enrichment cost, accuracy 18% → 97%), Cyera
  (88% of ~1,500 employees weekly), Anthropic (~80% Sales plugin adoption).
