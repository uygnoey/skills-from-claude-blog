# Partner statements and customer spotlight

Paraphrased from the Claude Marketplace announcement (2026-09-23).

## Partner statements

| Partner | Speaker | What they said (paraphrased) |
|---|---|---|
| Notion | David Rosenberg, Head of Ecosystem | The connector brings Notion's knowledge and context into Claude; the partnership advances Notion's goal of a shared home for people and AI. |
| Salesforce | Nick Johnston, EVP, Global Partnerships | Customers can reach CRM data and Slack conversations inside Claude; being in the marketplace helps more teams start faster. |
| Snowflake | Alexa Kelly, Head of Enterprise Technology & AI Partnerships | Applying committed spend makes Snowflake easier to adopt and shortens procurement cycles. |
| Atlassian | Jamil Valliani, Head of Product, AI | The Teamwork Graph connector brings context from Confluence, Jira, and other apps into Claude. |
| CrowdStrike | Daniel Bernard, Chief Business Officer | The Falcon platform is coming to the marketplace, bringing AI-native cybersecurity to enterprises. |
| Hebbia | Aabhas Sharma, President and CTO | Customers can use committed Anthropic spend to bring Hebbia to their teams. |
| Legora | Kristin Thayer, VP of Partnerships | Legal teams can use budget already committed to add Legora's aOS. |

## Customer spotlight

- **CodeRabbit** used part of its Anthropic commitment to pay for Vercel, where its coding agents run.
- **Power Digital** and **ThoughtSpot** used part of theirs for Snowflake, where their data lives.

The announcement links to a fuller write-up of these cases.
