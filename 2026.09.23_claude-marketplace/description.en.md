## What is this post?
The launch of the Claude Marketplace on September 23, 2026: one place that combines plugins and
connectors, Claude-powered agents and products, and service partners, for customers extending Claude
and for companies that want to reach Claude teams.

## When is it useful?
- When a team wants to find connectors, plugins, partner software, or consultants for Claude.
- When procurement wants to apply committed Anthropic spend to partner agents and products.
- When a vendor or consultancy wants to get listed.

## Key points
- Customers: 2,000+ connectors and plugins (Atlassian, Google, Microsoft, Notion, Salesforce, …).
- Customers can buy Claude-powered software (CrowdStrike, Cursor, Harvey, Legora, Lovable, Snowflake,
  …) with part of their committed Anthropic spend.
- Customers can engage Claude Partner Network firms (Accenture, BCG, Deloitte, …) for strategy and
  rollout.
- Builders: connectors/plugins are built with MCP and Agent Skills; agents/products apply to be
  listed; consultancies and SIs join the Claude Partner Network.
- Partner statements from Notion, Salesforce, Snowflake, Atlassian, CrowdStrike, Hebbia, and Legora;
  CodeRabbit used its commitment for Vercel, Power Digital and ThoughtSpot for Snowflake.
