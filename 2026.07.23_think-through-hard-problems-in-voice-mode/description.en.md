## What is this post?
Voice mode gets three upgrades at once: it now runs on Claude Opus and Claude Sonnet in addition to Haiku, it can act in your connected tools like Gmail and Slack, and it holds a conversation in eleven languages.

The framing is that some problems you can't type your way through — practicing for an important pitch meeting, deciding between multiple offers, reviewing your own process out loud, brainstorming. Voice mode previously ran only on Haiku for speed, which made it a fast responder rather than a thinking partner. Opus and Sonnet are models designed for hard problem-solving, and they are now available in voice.

## When is it useful?
- When you are rehearsing a conversation and want feedback on how you are communicating, not just on what you plan to say.
- When you want someone to find the gaps in an argument you already believe.
- When a brainstorm needs a check attached — ideas validated with competitive research rather than just listed.
- When a spoken session should end in a moved meeting, a drafted reply, or a one-pager.
- When you want to start out loud and finish in text without restarting the conversation.
- When picking between the Free and paid voice tiers.

## Key points
- **Voice mode now runs on Claude Opus, Sonnet, and Haiku.** It previously ran only on Haiku, chosen for speed; Opus and Sonnet are designed for hard problem-solving.
- **You can switch models mid-conversation** from the model picker, and voice mode uses the fastest version of whichever model you've selected so the conversation still runs smoothly.
- **It defaults to the last model you used in text chat**, so you can move between voice and text without starting over.
- **Claude can act in connected tools with your permission** — push a Google Calendar meeting by 30 minutes, turn a pitch conversation into a Canva one-pager, summarize received emails and draft responses to the critical ones. Connect tools under Settings > Connectors.
- **Eleven languages**: English, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazilian), Spanish (Latin America), Spanish (Spain). **Automatic detection is not available** — select manually or ask verbally.
- **Beta on mobile, desktop, and web, but works best from your phone.** Free plan: Haiku, one connected tool, all languages. Paid plans: expanded models and all connected tools. Voice conversations count toward regular usage limits.
