# Voice mode: a sounding board that keeps up

Some problems you can't type your way through. Voice mode is for practicing for an important pitch
meeting, deciding between multiple offers, reviewing your own process out loud, or brainstorming new
ideas.

This update does three things: it puts more capable models behind voice, it lets voice reach your
connected tools, and it adds languages.

## A sounding board that keeps up

Voice mode used to run only on Claude Haiku, chosen for speed. Claude Opus and Sonnet — models
designed for hard problem-solving — are now available in voice mode too.

You can switch models mid-conversation from the model picker. Voice mode uses the fastest version of
whichever model you've selected, so the conversation runs smoothly. And it defaults to the last model
you used in text chat, so you can move between voice and text without starting over.

That continuity is the part worth planning around. A session does not have to end when you stop
talking; it can continue as text in the same thread.

Four uses the announcement names:

- Practice for an important conversation and ask for feedback on how you're communicating.
- Talk through a client pitch and ask Claude to find gaps in your logic.
- Brainstorm ideas for your product roadmap and ask Claude to validate them with competitive research.
- Evaluate hypotheses for why your last video went viral.

The common shape: you supply the reasoning, and Claude's job is to push on it.

## From talking to doing

With your permission, Claude can act in your connected tools — Gmail, Slack, and the rest — so the
conversation can end in something done rather than something decided:

- Push a meeting on your Google Calendar by 30 minutes.
- Turn a conversation about a client pitch into a one-pager in Canva.
- Summarize received emails and draft responses to critical ones.

Connect tools under **Settings > Connectors** — before the session, so the action is available at the
moment you reach it.

## Converse in many more languages

Eleven languages are supported: English, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean,
Portuguese (Brazilian), Spanish (Latin America), and Spanish (Spain).

Automatic detection is not available, so select your language manually or ask for it verbally.

## Getting started

The latest update to voice mode is available in beta to all chat users on mobile, desktop, and web,
but works best from your phone.

On the **Free plan**, you get Claude Haiku, one connected tool, and all available languages. **Paid
plans** can access expanded models and all your connected tools. Voice conversations count toward your
regular usage limits.
