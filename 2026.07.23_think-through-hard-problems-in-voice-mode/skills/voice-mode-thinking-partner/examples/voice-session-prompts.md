# Voice session shapes

The four uses the post names, written out as sessions. The pattern in each is the same: you supply the
reasoning, and Claude's job is to push on it — not to produce the answer for you.

---

## 1. Rehearsal

**The situation.** An important conversation tomorrow — a pitch, a negotiation, a difficult piece of
feedback.

**The session.** Practice for an important conversation and request feedback on your communication
style. Say the thing you plan to say, out loud, the way you plan to say it. Then ask what landed and
what didn't.

**Why voice.** The delivery is the thing being reviewed, and delivery does not survive being typed.

**Ends in.** A second run-through. With a connector attached, the follow-up meeting moved on your
calendar.

---

## 2. Pressure-testing a pitch

**The situation.** A client pitch that you believe in, which is exactly the problem.

**The session.** Talk through the pitch and ask Claude to find gaps in your logic. Give the argument
in the order you plan to give it, and ask where a skeptical listener would stop you.

**Why voice.** You will explain it out loud in the room too, and the weak joints show up in speech
that do not show up on a slide.

**Ends in.** A revised order of argument. With connectors, the critical emails drafted before you put
the phone down.

---

## 3. Brainstorming, with a check attached

**The situation.** A product roadmap with more candidate bets than slots.

**The session.** Brainstorm ideas for your roadmap, then ask Claude to validate them with competitive
research. The second half matters: a brainstorm that never gets checked is just a list.

**Why voice.** Generation goes faster out loud, and the switch from generating to checking is one
sentence rather than a new document.

**Ends in.** A shortlist. With connectors, a one-pager in Canva.

---

## 4. Post-mortem on a result

**The situation.** Something worked and you don't know why — the last video went viral.

**The session.** Evaluate your hypotheses for why. State each one, and ask Claude which the evidence
actually supports and what would distinguish them.

**Why voice.** Hypotheses are cheap to say and expensive to write down; you will generate more of them
talking.

**Ends in.** The one or two hypotheses worth testing on the next post.

---

## A note on continuity

Voice mode defaults to the last model you used in text chat, and you can move between voice and text
without starting over. So the natural end of any of these sessions is not "done" — it is opening the
same thread in text and writing up what you decided.
