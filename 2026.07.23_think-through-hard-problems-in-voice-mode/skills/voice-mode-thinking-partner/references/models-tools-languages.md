# Models, tools, languages, and plans

Everything the post specifies about what voice mode now supports.

## Models

Voice mode previously ran only on **Claude Haiku**, chosen for speed. It now runs on:

- **Claude Opus** — designed for hard problem-solving
- **Claude Sonnet** — designed for hard problem-solving
- **Claude Haiku** — the fast option, and the only model on the Free plan

Behavior worth knowing:

- **Switch mid-conversation** from the model picker.
- Voice mode uses the **fastest version of whichever model you've selected**, so the conversation
  keeps running smoothly.
- It **defaults to the last model you used in text chat**, so you can move between voice and text
  without starting over.

## Connected tools

Connect tools under **Settings > Connectors**. Connected tools named in the post include **Gmail** and
**Slack**; the actions given as examples are:

| You say | Claude does |
| --- | --- |
| "Push my 2pm by half an hour" | Moves the meeting on your Google Calendar by 30 minutes |
| "Make this a one-pager" | Turns the conversation about the client pitch into a one-pager in Canva |
| "What came in today?" | Summarizes received emails and drafts responses to the critical ones |

Claude executes actions in connected tools **with your permission**.

## Languages

Eleven supported languages:

- English
- French
- German
- Hindi
- Indonesian
- Italian
- Japanese
- Korean
- Portuguese (Brazilian)
- Spanish (Latin America)
- Spanish (Spain)

**Automatic language detection is not available.** Select your language manually, or ask for it
verbally.

## Availability and plans

- **Beta**, for all chat users on **mobile, desktop, and web** — and it **works best from your phone**.
- **Free plan**: Claude Haiku, one connected tool, all available languages.
- **Paid plans**: expanded models and all your connected tools.
- **Voice conversations count toward your regular usage limits.**
