## What is this post?
The announcement that HCLTech (230,000+ people in 60 countries) joined the Claude Partner Network as
a Global Premier partner and will bring Claude to banks, hospitals, telecom companies, and
manufacturers with controls for high-stakes environments.

## When is it useful?
- When a systems integrator or ISV plans its own Claude practice.
- When a regulated client asks how Claude will be tested and overseen before production.

## Key points
- Track 1: an Anthropic Business Unit and Center of Excellence. Forward-deployed engineers build
  secure sandboxes for clients to test Claude on their own systems and data, train client
  workforces, and build controls for agents at scale.
- Track 2: HCLSoftware becomes an Anthropic ISV partner, starting with HCL BigFix (endpoint
  management), HCL AppScan (application security testing), and the Actian AI analyst (data and
  analytics).
- Track 3: Claude and Claude Code in AI Force (IT support, code remediation, digital employee
  services) with policy guardrails, exception handling, and human oversight; engineers approve
  anything affecting client production systems.
- Quotes from C Vijayakumar (HCLTech) and Daniela Amodei (Anthropic) on scale, product depth, and
  trust.
