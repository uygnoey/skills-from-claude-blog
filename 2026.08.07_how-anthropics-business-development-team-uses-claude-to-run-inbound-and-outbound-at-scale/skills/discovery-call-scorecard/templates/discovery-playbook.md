# Discovery call playbook

The scorecard skill "evaluates Gong transcripts against our discovery call
playbook." That playbook is the team's own — the post does not publish its
contents. What this template provides is a structure that a scoring skill can
actually check against, since the scorecard requires an explicit pass or fail
"on our criteria."

---

## How to write a checkable criterion

A criterion works if a reader with only the transcript can decide whether it was
met. Prefer statements about **what happened in the conversation** over
statements about how the call felt.

- Checkable: "The customer stated what happens if the problem is not solved."
- Not checkable: "The rep built rapport."

Each criterion needs: what must occur, what evidence in a transcript shows it,
and what near-miss does not count.

## Criterion template

### _[Area of the call]_

**Criterion:** _[what must happen]_

**Evidence in transcript:** _[what a scorer should be able to point at]_

**Does not count:** _[the near-miss]_

**Weight:** _[does failing this fail the call?]_

---

## Suggested areas to cover

Fill these with your team's actual standards:

1. **Opening and framing** — what the rep establishes before questions start.
2. **Problem discovery** — what must be surfaced about the customer's situation.
3. **Impact and consequence** — what must be established about why it matters.
4. **Environment and constraints** — what has to be understood about how they
   work today.
5. **Decision process** — who is involved, what has to happen next.
6. **Listening behavior** — how the rep handles what the customer actually said.
7. **Close and next step** — what must be agreed before the call ends.

## Pass/fail

Decide in advance which criteria are disqualifying on their own and which
contribute. The scorecard states the verdict *and* the criterion that decided it,
so the boundary has to exist before scoring starts.

## Versioning

Record which version of the playbook produced a scorecard. When the playbook
changes, past scores mean something different — and reps will notice before the
tooling does.
