# Discovery call scorecard

The four sections below are exactly the ones the post specifies: "the feedback
includes top three things done well, top three areas to improve, an explicit
pass or fail score on our criteria, and a single highest-leverage thing to
practice next." Field layout is a working aid; the four parts and their counts
come from the source.

---

## Notes on use

- **Three and three, not everything.** The counts are the discipline. A list of
  nine improvements is a list nobody acts on.
- **Quotes, not characterizations.** The post's word is "specific feedback based
  on the conversation."
- **One practice item.** Not a plan — one thing, for the next call.
