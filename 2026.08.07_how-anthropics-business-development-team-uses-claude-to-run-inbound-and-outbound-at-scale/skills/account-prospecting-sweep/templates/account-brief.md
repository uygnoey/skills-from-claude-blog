# Account brief, score, and play

The post describes the morning artifact of the overnight sweep: "In the morning,
I open up Claude Cowork to a brief, a score, and an outbound play for each
account."

This template gives that artifact a shape. The three parts and their inputs come
from the post; the field-level layout is a working aid.

---

## Notes on use

- The brief exists so the rep can walk into a conversation "informed on their
  business and close enough to their problems to have a deeper strategic
  discussion," as the post puts it.
- The ledger section is what keeps the next overnight run from proposing the
  same play again.
- Feedback is not a comment box: the post has rep feedback flowing back into the
  skill itself.
