# Role scenarios from the post

| Role | Question | What Claude returns | Format | Time noted in post |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Corporate strategy lead | Brief on a competitor | Positioning, market share, product capabilities, recent acquisitions, cited to IDC research | Brief | Minutes instead of a day |
| CIO evaluating procurement software | Best-fit vendors given existing architecture and business requirements | Top three vendors from IDC's procurement database and MarketScape research | Excel scorecard for the evaluation team | - |
| CFO | How next year's IT budget compares to IDC's forecast (budget uploaded) | Where the two differ and by how much; uploaded data stays private | Comparison | - |
| Market intelligence professional | What a competitor's acquisition means for the business | Board-ready analysis grounded in IDC research | Executive brief | Minutes instead of hours |

## Where it works
Claude app on desktop and mobile, Cowork, and the Microsoft Office add-ins.

## What makes the answers trustworthy (per the post)
- Claude works from the same data and definitions as an IDC analyst.
- Each answer cites the IDC document or dataset it came from.
