## What is this post?
An announcement from October 28, 2026. IDC, a technology intelligence firm with more than 60 years of
history, worked with Anthropic to bring IDC Quanta into Claude as an MCP connector, available to IDC
customers now. IDC has also rolled Claude out across its own company.

## When is it useful?
- You have IDC Quanta access and want finished analysis (briefs, shortlists, benchmarks) instead of reading
  long reports.
- You want market numbers in Claude that match IDC's definitions and come with citations.
- You plan a company-wide Claude rollout and want a concrete sequence and results from a research firm.

## Key points
- Customers describe the market, strategy, or purchase they are working on, and the IDC Quanta connector
  returns analysis built on IDC's forecasts, market data, and research. Uses include benchmarking, market
  sizing, vendor selection, and strategy.
- Example scenarios: a strategy lead gets a cited competitor brief in minutes instead of a day; a CIO gets
  the top three procurement vendors from IDC data and MarketScape research as an Excel scorecard; a CFO
  uploads an IT budget and sees how it differs from IDC's forecast, with the uploaded data kept private.
- It works in the Claude desktop and mobile apps, Cowork, and the Microsoft Office add-ins. Claude uses the
  same data and definitions as IDC analysts, and each answer cites its IDC source.
- Internal adoption: engineering went first. Engineers rebuilt an internal research data repository in
  three weeks instead of an estimated three months, and shipped-code bugs fell after using Claude Code for
  testing. Code review went from five days to about 30 minutes.
- Product managers prototype with Claude Code (months to weeks before reaching engineering); Cowork drafts
  plans (planning sprints from three weeks to three days); writing a new test takes about 20 minutes; a
  Claude-built tool reads CRM exports to flag stalled deals; a content assistant drafts marketing content
  with SEO/AEO and persona checks before human editing.
- IDC's CEO says acquisition-impact analysis now takes minutes, grounded in more than 15 billion data points.
