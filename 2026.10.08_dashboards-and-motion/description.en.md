## What is this post?
An announcement of two betas and one graduation. Claude Dashboards (beta, paid plans) turns a
plain-language question about company data into a dashboard that stays current. Claude Motion (beta,
Team and Enterprise) turns an idea or report into an animated explainer built as editable code.
Claude Docs, Slides, and Design leave beta and are available on every plan, including Free, and the
standalone Claude Design site is being folded into Claude.

## When is it useful?
- When you want a quick answer from a data warehouse or CRM without filing a ticket or writing SQL.
- When an idea would land better as a short animation than as another static slide.
- When an Enterprise admin is planning to enable these features or migrate Claude Design systems.

## Key points
- Dashboards connect to Amazon Redshift, BigQuery, ClickHouse, Databricks, Snowflake, and other
  connectors such as Salesforce. Click any number to see its query; each chart shows its last refresh.
- Dashboards can be sent to Amplitude, Grafana, Hex, Mixpanel, Omni, Perplexity, PostHog, or Sigma for
  deeper analysis (Databricks Genie, Looker, monday.com, Tableau coming soon).
- Motion writes code that animates text, charts, shapes, and images; export as MP4 or open in Adobe,
  Descript, HeyGen, Higgsfield, invideo, Luma AI, or Runway. No video generation model, no generated
  footage or AI-generated people.
- Docs, Slides, and Design (45M+ made) go GA: CMEK for artifacts, admin-chosen templates, co-editing,
  external/link sharing if allowed, faithful PowerPoint/PDF export, editable Google Slides export,
  and mobile editing.
- The standalone claude.ai/design stays up until December 14; migrate team design systems from the
  Artifacts page. Chats, comments, and public links in the standalone version end when it closes.
- Enterprise: Dashboards and Motion are off by default (Organization settings > Artifacts); Docs,
  Slides, and Design turn on by default October 15.
